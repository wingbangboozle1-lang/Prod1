Meat thermometers

Instant-read or wireless? It is a question about distance

If you are standing at the grill, a one-second read in your hand is the whole job. If a brisket is going for six hours while you do something else, you want a probe that reaches your phone. That is the decision. Everything below is what the two listings state, and one of them never states an accuracy figure at all.

Accuracy is a claim with a source, or it is marketing

The instant-read listing states ±0.5°F and names NIST and NSF in its specification field. The wireless listing names twenty standards in one field and states no tolerance anywhere in the text we read. Both are seller statements. We have calibrated neither unit, so we print the claim and the gap, and we do not put the two on an accuracy ranking - that would need a measurement we have not made.
