Design brief: suggestions for the publication redesign

Everything on this page is a suggestion for the design prompt, not a decision. A reviewer picks a direction, and only approved items are implemented. The brief is written so it can be fed to a design tool as-is; each block states what it changes, what it needs, and what decision it blocks.

3. Anti-AI-slop writing rules (apply to every page)

  • No filler openers ("In today's fast-paced world"), no "in conclusion", no em-dash chains of three.
  • One idea per paragraph; vary sentence length; prefer the specific number over the adjective.
  • Every product claim links to its Amazon listing; missing data is left blank and flagged, never filled with a guess.
  • Seller claims are attributed ("per listing"); our own words stay first-person and honest about what we have and have not tested.
  • No superlatives, no "best", "top-rated" or "#1" claims; popularity badges appear only as dated listing snapshots.

5. Improvements for decision-making

  • Keep this suggestions board as the single review surface: every change starts as an SG item, a reviewer approves, then it is implemented and re-verified.
  • Keep the verification register and flagged page linked from the footer so readers and reviewers can audit any claim.
  • Measure before optimising: once analytics is live (SG-03), choose the next topic from measured queries, not instinct.
  • Gate the manual parts: Special Link testing, photography licensing and domain choice stay owner decisions, recorded here.

Nothing here is implemented until approved. Irregularities and unverified items stay on Flagged for review. The decision questions this brief answers are listed on the Suggestions board (Q-09 to Q-14).
