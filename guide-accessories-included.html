What's actually in the box: accessories, per the listing

By The Countertop Guide editorial desk · Listing data checked 2026-11-20 · Spec-based, not hands-on tested how we work

The accessory list decides what you can cook on day one and what you will need to replace. Here is what each verified listing includes, stated as listed - including one count the listing itself gives two ways.

On this page: The picks – Full comparison – Questions readers ask

Air fryers

  • Chefman 6QT dual (B0CDY277D3): two 3-quart non-stick baskets, per the listing.
  • Chefman 8QT (B08DKYBTPH): an 8-quart non-stick basket and a flat frying tray, per the listing.
  • Emeril 26QT oven (B09B7SB46R): the title says six cooking accessories included; the listing's Item-details table lists seven. Both counts are the listing's and disagree - see FLG-021. Count on the listing, not the headline.

Thermometers

  • ThermoMaven instant-read (B0DHZTCNR5): includes a calibration certificate, per the listing's included-components field.
  • CHEF iQ Sense wireless (B0DZFD6R9Z): three ultra-thin probes plus a hub that charges all three at once, per the listing. Probe count is sold as variants - pick the count you need on the listing.

Why the count matters

Accessories are the parts that wear and the parts you replace. A title count and a table count that disagree is exactly the detail to resolve on the listing before you buy, because a missing tray or an extra probe changes what you can cook on day one.

What the listing cannot tell you

The listing states what is included, not how each accessory is cared for, whether replacements are stocked, or whether a given accessory fits other models. Check the care instructions and the replacement availability on the listing before relying on any one part.
