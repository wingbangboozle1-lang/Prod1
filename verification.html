Claim audit: every superlative the site prints, tested twice

A register that is checked while its prose is not will drift: someone corrects a number and a phrase like “the narrowest” stays standing there. So each comparative or superlative on a shop page is tested twice on every build. First against the register - the ordering or the figure has to follow from data/products.json, recomputed here rather than transcribed. Then against the HTML this build just wrote - the sentence the audit describes has to still be on the page it is claimed to be on. Either failure stops the build.
