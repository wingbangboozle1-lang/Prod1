Will a 26-quart oven fit your counter? The depth and height math

By The Countertop Guide editorial desk · Listing data checked 2026-11-20 · Spec-based, not hands-on tested how we work

Counter fit is two measurements, not one. Depth decides whether the appliance sits under the cabinet; height decides whether it clears the cabinet above. Both come from the listed dimensions - and here is the arithmetic, so you can run it on any listing.

On this page: The picks – Full comparison – Questions readers ask

Depth: the number that decides under-cabinet fit

From the listed dimensions (depth × width × height, inches):

  • Chefman 8QT basket: 12.75D × 13.5W × 10H in — 32.4 cm deep.
  • Chefman 6QT dual-basket: 13.38D × 11.81W × 10.63H in — 34.0 cm deep.
  • Emeril 26QT oven: 16.9D × 15.8W × 14.8H in — 42.9 cm deep, and 37.6 cm tall.

Inch to centimetre is a single multiply: depth in × 2.54 = depth in cm. The Emeril needs about 10 cm more depth than the Chefman 8QT. That gap, not the quarts, is what usually decides fit.

Height: the number people forget

Depth gets the appliance onto the counter; height decides whether it clears the cabinet above. The Emeril is 14.8 in (37.6 cm) tall, against 10.63 in (27.0 cm) for the Chefman 6QT and 10 in (25.4 cm) for the Chefman 8QT. If you have a wall cabinet or a window above, measure the clear space, not the counter.

Footprint: how much counter it owns

Footprint is depth × width, derived from the same listed dimensions: Emeril 267.0 sq in, Chefman 8QT 172.1 sq in, Chefman 6QT dual 158.0 sq in. It is arithmetic we do from the listing, not a manufacturer figure.

Run the math yourself

  1. Measure your counter depth and the clear height above it, in inches.
  2. Compare both against the listed depth and height for the model you are considering.
  3. Add door-swing clearance for French-door models; the listing dimensions describe the box, not the door arc.

What the listing cannot tell you

The listed dimensions describe the appliance, not your kitchen. They do not include door swing, handle depth, the power cord, or the real usable cavity. We have not measured any of these units; the figures above are the listing's, converted and multiplied, with the method shown so you can repeat it.

Questions readers ask

How deep is the Emeril 26QT oven?

16.9 inches (42.9 cm) deep and 14.8 inches (37.6 cm) tall, per the Amazon listing dimensions (16.9D x 15.8W x 14.8H in). Measure your counter and the clearance above it before buying.

Which of the three takes the least counter depth?

The Chefman 8QT basket at 12.75 inches (32.4 cm) deep, per the listing. The Chefman 6QT dual is 13.38 inches (34.0 cm); the Emeril 26QT is 16.9 inches (42.9 cm).

Do the listed dimensions include door swing?

No. Listed dimensions describe the appliance box. French-door models like the Emeril need extra clearance for the doors to open; measure that separately.
