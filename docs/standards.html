COUNTER & CRUMB · AFFILIATE & EDITORIAL POLICY

Clear links. Clear limits. Reader first.

We aim to help readers make informed choices, not manufacture certainty. This page describes the current publication standards and what remains to be decided.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. We repeat this disclosure near the top of every page and in the site footer. Individual affiliate links are marked rel="sponsored" when they are published; the submitted product links are not currently promoted from the register as product recommendations.

Official guidance: Amazon Associates Operating Agreement · Program Policies · FTC disclosure guidance · FTC endorsement guidance.

Editorial rules

  • Separate confirmed facts, reader questions, editorial judgment, and unverified information.
  • Link factual product claims to authoritative sources and record dates for changing information.
  • Do not invent ratings, prices, popularity, trend status, test outcomes, commissions, or conversion rates.
  • Never imply hands-on use or endorsement without the real experience and context.
  • Correct errors transparently; do not hide uncertainty.

Links and measurement

Published outbound affiliate links must carry rel="sponsored" and remain clear about their destination. Amazon link and API use is subject to current official terms. No product data is pulled from an Amazon API in this static site.

The site currently has no server-side analytics. The small browser script increments a local count only; it does not send data to us. This is not a site-wide click counter or conversion measurement. See campaign ledger.

Images

The homepage artwork is CSS illustration, not product photography. Product photography will require authorized, accurate source material and a separate review.
