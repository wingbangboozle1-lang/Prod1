COUNTER & CRUMB · METHOD & MEASUREMENT

A small evidence trail beats a confident guess

We publish only what the available evidence can support. The current repository has no verified product catalog, test results, search data, affiliate reports, or analytics service.

Evidence tiers

  1. Verified official source: directly checked against a relevant official source, with date and specific link.
  2. First-party observation: directly tested or measured by the publication; protocol and limits disclosed.
  3. Submitted, not verified: supplied input retained as such, never silently elevated to fact.
  4. Unknown: blank until evidence exists; surfaced on the flagged page.

Product-intent record

For each candidate, retain intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date, confirmation source, and observed redirect target as separate fields. Never overwrite intended destination with the observed redirect.

Measurement status

Events we propose defining: guide_view, outbound_click, chooser_complete, search_landing, and reported_order. Only outbound clicks are locally counted in the visitor’s own browser. The remaining event definitions are proposed; none are transmitted or reported. Reported orders must come only from authorized first-party reports, with timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks, and orders plus dated provenance. There is no score or revenue forecast.

Google’s Search Console getting-started guide describes site verification and search monitoring. Search data is not connected here. Google’s people-first content guidance informs our editorial principle, not a claim of ranking performance.

Search and publishing checklist

  • Give every page a unique title and description.
  • Publish only completed, useful pages and add them to sitemap and feed.
  • Verify canonical domain, sitemap access, robots rules, and Search Console ownership when the final deployment URL is confirmed.
  • Use a dated editorial calendar; review actual query data before deciding the next topic.
  • Do not promise ranking, traffic, or conversion outcomes.
