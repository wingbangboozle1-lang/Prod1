Findings

  1. All five short links resolved to amazon.com product pages on 2026-11-19 (intent record). This is an observation, not a validation of the tracking.
  2. The slugs are not ASINs. Each slug is 9 characters; the observed ASINs are 10 characters (F-01).
  3. The five products fall into three categories: two basket-style air fryers and one French-door air-fryer oven under "Fryers > Air Fryers", and two thermometers under "Thermometers & Timers > Meat". The breadcrumb is the source for each.
  4. Capacity is stated in different units. The titles say "6 Quart", "8 Qt" and "26QT", and the 6-quart listing also gives "2 Independent 3Qt" baskets. The guide on capacity explains why these figures are not directly comparable.
  5. Counter-space analysis cannot be done yet. The listing dimensions were not captured, so no capacity-per-depth figure is published (S-23).
