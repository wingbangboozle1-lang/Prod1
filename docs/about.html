About, method and disclosure

Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. This site is not endorsed by or affiliated with Amazon. The Associates Operating Agreement, Section 5, asks for this statement to be clearly and prominently displayed. It is shown under the masthead on every page and repeated in the footer. Sources: Operating Agreement; FTC Disclosures 101.

Sponsored links

Every link to an Amazon page is marked rel="sponsored". Source: Google Search Central.

How product facts are recorded

  • Each submitted link is opened and the final URL and page title are recorded, as observed.
  • Intended ASIN, confirmation date, price and rating are kept in separate fields. If a field cannot be confirmed from an official source, it stays blank and is marked Unverified.
  • Claims that depend on popularity or trends are not made. Guide titles are working titles.

Measurement

Five events are defined: page_view, outbound_click, guide_read_75, category_click, feed_click. They are counted in the visitor's browser only. No cookies, no third-party scripts, and no personal data are used. A self-hosted endpoint is provided in the repository, but it is not wired to this site until an owner decides where it runs (FLAG-06).

Publishing gate

The build (scripts/build.mjs) checks every page before publication: the disclosure is above the main content and repeated in the footer; every Amazon link is sponsored; every internal link resolves; each page has a unique title and description; banned claim words are absent; the sitemap lists every page; and the product table has a source on every row.
