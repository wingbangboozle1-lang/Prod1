About

Countertop Check is a working name for this site (FL-14). It reads five Amazon listings closely and reports what each one says.

Method

  • Only fields confirmed from a page we read are shown. Everything else is blank and flagged.
  • Guides use the listing wording and say what they do not know.
  • Suggestions and open questions are listed for review. Nothing is changed on the strength of a suggestion alone.

Byline

Articles are credited to the “Editorial team”. No individual author is named. A named author can be added after a decision (S-03).
