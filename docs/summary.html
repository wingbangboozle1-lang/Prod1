Executive summary

What the site is, what has been verified, what has not, and what the owner needs to decide.

What was built

  • A static site of 5 product listings in two categories, four question-shaped guides, and pages for analysis, sources, flags, suggestions, questions, measurement and policy. All are linked from the footer.
  • Every product row links to its official Amazon listing. Every outbound product link carries the tracking ID shopnow047c-20 and rel="sponsored".
  • The disclosure required by Associates Operating Agreement section 5 appears under the masthead of every page and again in the footer.
  • A build script and a gate (tools/check_site.py) regenerate the site from the data files and fail the build on a missing disclosure, a sponsored-link error, or a broken internal link.

What has been verified

  • The five submitted short links each resolve to one Amazon listing (ASINs B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z), checked 2026-11-19.
  • None of the five resolved URLs carries a tag= parameter. This matches the earlier Zone4 audit (S15, context only).
  • Associates Operating Agreement section 5 wording and Participation Requirements section 2(a)-(b) were read directly (S1, S2). Google's sponsored-link guidance (S3) and FTC placement guidance (S4) were read.

What is not verified, and why

  • Tracking ID status. The ID was supplied; it has not been confirmed as the enrolled Partner Tag (P-01).
  • Special Links. The one-link-per-product test in Associates Central has not been run (P-02).
  • Intended ASINs. Read from the resolved listings; the owner has not confirmed them (P-03).
  • Prices, availability, ratings. Not shown; the policy limits them (P-05).
  • Commission rates and earnings. Not shown; the per-ASIN category is unknown (P-06).
  • Analytics and conversions. Not measured. GitHub Pages cannot host the requested endpoint (P-10). No conversion score or revenue forecast is made.
  • Trends and popularity. No claim is made; no dated first-party data exists (P-19).
