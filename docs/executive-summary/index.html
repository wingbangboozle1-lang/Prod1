Executive summary

A static, sponsored-link product site for five submitted Amazon listings, built from verified fields only. Two things keep it from being a complete affiliate operation: the link format and the tracking attribution are unconfirmed, and several decisions are still open.

What exists

  • A front page, a products table, two category pages, five guides, and dashboards for analysis, verification, the campaign ledger, measurement, suggestions, open questions, Flagged for Review, and this summary.
  • Every outbound product link is marked rel="sponsored", following Google's documentation. The disclosure strip sits under the masthead of every page and is repeated in the footer.

What was verified

  • All five submitted links resolve to amazon.com listings. Observed ASINs: B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z.
  • Titles and breadcrumbs were read from each listing. Capacity and accuracy values come only from titles.
  • Amazon's Operating Agreement section 5 disclosure wording is confirmed and used.
  • The FTC Disclosures 101 brochure was read. It asks for disclosures that are hard to miss and placed with the endorsement (FL-10).

What is not verified (left blank, flagged)

  • Intended ASINs: the submitted codes are 9 characters, and none has been confirmed as intended (FL-01, FL-02).
  • Tracking attribution: none of the five redirect URLs carries tag=shopnow047c-20 (FL-04).
  • Special Link status of the short links: unresolved, not claimed invalid; requires an Associates test (FL-05).
  • Prices, ratings, dimensions, commission rates (FL-06, FL-08, FL-16).

What needs a decision

See Suggestions (ten items, options only). The first is S-01: test one Special Link per product before scaling. Brand, byline, cadence, analytics endpoint, imagery and price display are also open.

What is not possible from here

  • Logging in to Associates or Search Console (manual).
  • Running the analytics endpoint on static hosting (needs a server you choose).
  • Reading the external source register in Zone3 / Zone4 (not in this repository; FL-12).

Publishing cadence and measurement

Cadence has not been set (S-04). Guide topics should be chosen from measured queries once Search Console and the endpoint have data for 8 to 12 weeks (Q-07).
