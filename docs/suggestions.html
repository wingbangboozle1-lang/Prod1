COUNTER & CRUMB · IDEAS & DECISIONS

A review desk for the next right move

A transparent suggestion list separates known facts from hypotheses. Each proposal includes the question to settle, the evidence currently available, and a practical way to discover what is missing.

How to use this page

These are proposals, not decisions or research findings. Pick a direction only after its open questions have answers. Do not treat the sample article titles as evidence of search demand.

Editorial hypotheses to test—not popularity claims

Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Compare only verified format, dimensions, capacity, cleaning instructions, and household constraints. No winner absent transparent evidence.

A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Explain measurement units, usable space where verifiable, and why listed capacity alone may not answer a buyer’s needs.

How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

Offer a tape-measure checklist; do not claim tested cleanup experience without actual testing.

Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

Build a neutral decision checklist from verified functions and reader priorities.

Suggestions for the decision-maker

  1. Approve the evidence gate first. Verify one product link end-to-end before scaling.
  2. Choose a content standard. Prefer firsthand testing, explicit limitations, dated official facts, and an author/editor review.
  3. Set a cadence after capacity is known. A sustainable starting proposal is one thoroughly checked guide every two weeks, then reassess after 8–12 weeks of data. This is an operational suggestion, not a traffic promise.
  4. Define measurement and privacy. Decide hosting, data minimization, retention, consent, and access before enabling analytics.
  5. Approve a photo policy. Use original, authorized photography; never represent generated imagery as product photography.

Decision record

No product ranking, revenue forecast, search-volume claim, trend claim, or conversion optimization decision is made here because supporting evidence is absent. Record the chosen path, reviewer, date, evidence, and any dissent before changing that status.
