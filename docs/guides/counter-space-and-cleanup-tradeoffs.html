How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

Every appliance takes up space and needs cleaning. Measure both before you pay.

Editorial team (no named author; see FLAG-08) · Published 2026-11-19 · Working title

Measure before you shop

Write down the free width, depth and height where the appliance will sit, including room for the lid, door or drawer to open and for the cord to reach a socket. Compare those figures with the dimensions printed on the listing.

Questions about cleanup

  • Are the baskets, trays or racks dishwasher-safe, as the listing states?
  • Does the interior have corners or crevices that need a brush?
  • Where will you wash the parts, and does the sink or dishwasher have room?

Questions about counter space

  • Will you leave it out, or store it in a cupboard after each use?
  • Is there clearance above the unit for venting?
  • Can you lift and move it safely with one hand?

Write the trade-off down

Two columns, one for cleanup and one for space, and a row for each product. The question is which trade-off you can live with, not which product wins on paper.
