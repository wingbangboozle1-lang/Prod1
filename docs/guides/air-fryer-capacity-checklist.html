A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity is printed in several units. Compare them the same way before you decide.

Editorial team (no named author; see FLAG-08) · Published 2026-11-19 · Working title

Units are not the same

Listings give capacity in quarts, in litres, or per basket. A figure for the whole machine is not the same as a figure per basket. Write each figure down with its unit before you compare two machines.

The checklist

  • Is the capacity for the whole machine or for each basket?
  • Does the listing state the unit, or do you need to convert?
  • How many portions of your usual food fit in one batch?
  • Does the figure in the title match the figure in the specifications?
  • Did you record the figure and the date you read it?

Where capacity figures come from

Use the official listing page as the source for the figure, and record the date you read it. If two places disagree, the listing page is the reference for this site until the manufacturer's own documents are checked.
