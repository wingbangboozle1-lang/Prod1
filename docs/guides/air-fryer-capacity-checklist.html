A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity numbers look simple until you compare them. Units, basket counts, and size variants can all change the answer.

By the Editorial team (no named author; see flag P-13)

Check the unit first

The listing titles in our table use 'Quart' (6 Quart, 8 Qt) and 'QT' (26QT). Those are not the same as litres. Convert both values to the same unit yourself before you compare them. This site does not convert units.

Checklist

  • Is the figure per basket or for the whole appliance? The 6 Quart listing's title gives both a 6 Quart figure and 3Qt per basket. Read which one the listing means.
  • Does the link open the size you mean? Our 8 Qt short link resolves to a listing page with a variation parameter (th=1). Other size options were not checked. Confirm the size on the page before you buy.
  • Match capacity to a typical meal, not to the largest number on the page.
  • Write the capacity you need in your own units, then compare listings against that note.
  • Record the date you checked. Listings change.

What this guide does not say

This checklist does not say which capacity is best. The right size depends on your cooking.
