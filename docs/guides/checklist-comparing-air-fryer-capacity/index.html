A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

By Counter Notes editorial (author to be named). Published 2026-11-19. Facts checked on 2026-11-19 against the listings cited below.

Capacity is the number most often compared and the one most often misread. The air fryers in our table state capacity in different ways. This checklist shows how to compare them on the same basis before you trust any single figure.

How the listings state capacity

  • Chefman 6 Quart Dual Basket: "6 Quart" in the title, plus "2 Independent 3Qt" baskets.
  • Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry: "8 Qt" in the title, with one "XL Nonstick Dishwasher-Safe Basket".
  • Emeril Lagasse 26QT: "26QT" in the title, with "Extra Large Interior".

A total-quart figure and a per-basket figure are not the same measure. A "6 Quart" unit with two 3-quart baskets, for example, is described two ways in the same listing. Check which measure each listing uses before comparing.

The checklist

  1. Find the basis. Is the figure total capacity, per basket, or a cooking-volume estimate? If the listing does not say, mark it as unknown.
  2. Use one unit. Compare quarts with quarts. Do not mix a total figure against a per-basket figure.
  3. Test against your food. Imagine your largest batch. Does it fit the stated capacity in one layer, or does it need two cooks?
  4. Read the title for stated specs only. Titles repeat the manufacturer's claims. They do not verify them.
  5. Note what is missing. If dimensions, weight or basket size are not on the listing, write that down rather than estimating.
  6. Keep a record. Note the listing URL, the date you checked it, and the figure exactly as written.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming a bigger quart number always means more usable space.
  • Comparing an oven-style unit's capacity with a basket's without accounting for shape.
  • Using a number from a review or forum instead of the official listing.

Limits of this checklist

This guide does not claim that any listed capacity is accurate. It shows how to compare the figures as written. We have not measured any product.
