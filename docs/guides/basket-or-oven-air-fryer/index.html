Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

By Editorial team · Published 2026-11-19 · Checked against listings 2026-11-19

Most of the air fryers we track come in two shapes. A basket model uses a drawer you pull out and shake. An oven-style model is a countertop box with a door, racks, and often a second heating zone. The shape changes almost everything else, so compare the shape first.

Questions to ask before you compare brands

  • How many people usually eat from one batch? A single basket fills faster than a multi-rack oven.
  • Do you bake, roast or toast as well as fry? Oven-style models list more functions; check which ones the listing names.
  • Where will it sit? Measure the counter depth and height yourself, because the listings in this study did not confirm dimensions.
  • Will you clean it by hand or in the dishwasher? Check the listing for dishwasher-safe parts before you assume it.

What the listings in this study state

Our listing records (see the Products table) show a 6 Quart dual-basket model, an 8 Qt model whose title lists a 4-in-1 function set and one basket, and a 26 QT oven-style model with 24 one-touch presets. What this guide does not tell you
