How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

By Editorial team · Published 2026-11-19 · Checked against listings 2026-11-19

Two things decide whether an appliance stays on the counter or in the cupboard: how long cleaning takes, and how much space it takes up. Neither shows up clearly in a headline capacity figure.

Cleanup questions

  • Are the baskets, trays and racks listed as dishwasher-safe? Check the title and the product details.
  • How many removable parts does the model have? Count them from the product photos on the listing, not from memory.
  • Is there a nonstick coating, and does the listing describe its material? Record exactly what it says.

Counter-space questions

  • Measure the spot you have: width, depth and clearance above the unit, including the space to open a door or pull a drawer.
  • Compare that with the dimensions in the product details table. This site did not confirm dimensions for the five products, so it does not publish a figure.
  • Ask whether you need the space for the appliance all the time, or only on the days you use it.

A worksheet

Write down three numbers for each candidate: its listed capacity, its listed dimensions, and the number of parts you would wash. Put them side by side. The trade-off usually shows up in that table, not in the headline.

Limits of this guide

This guide does not calculate capacity per centimetre of counter depth. That calculation needs confirmed dimensions, which are still missing (see Open questions Q-06).
