How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

Cleanup and footprint are the two things a product photo rarely answers. Here is how to check them from the listing itself.

By the Editorial team (no named author; see flag P-13)

Cleanup

Read the cleaning claims as written. Our tracked titles mention dishwasher-safe baskets for the Chefman 6 Quart (Dishwasher Safe Frying Baskets) and the Chefman 8 Qt (XL Nonstick Dishwasher-Safe Basket). The Emeril title does not mention dishwasher use, so do not assume it either way; check the listing's own text.

Counter space

Footprint depends on the appliance's stated dimensions and on how much clearance you need around it. Our table leaves dimensions blank until they are taken from an official listing field. Measure your own counter and compare it with the listing's figures.

Trade-offs to write down

  • Write down each model's listed dimensions in the same units before you compare footprints.
  • Check whether the listed dimensions include the door or the handle swing, and allow for it.
  • Count the parts you will wash, not just the ones the listing says are dishwasher-safe.
  • Note any wash step that cannot go in a dishwasher, and how often you will do it.

What this guide does not say

No footprint, cleaning time, or ease-of-use rating is stated for any product.
