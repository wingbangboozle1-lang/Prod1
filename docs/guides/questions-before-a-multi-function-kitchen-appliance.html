Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

One box that air fries, bakes, toasts and dehydrates can replace several. It can also replace none of them well.

Editorial team (no named author; see FLAG-08) · Published 2026-11-19 · Working title

List the jobs first

Write down the five things you cook most often. A multi-function appliance is worth a closer look only if it covers most of those jobs.

Questions to ask

  • Which functions are on the listing, and which are only in the title?
  • How many preset options does the listing state, and how many would you use?
  • What accessories are included, and what would you have to buy separately?
  • Does the listing state a maximum temperature, and is it in the unit you use?
  • What does the warranty cover, and where is it written?

Read the title and the specifications together

A long title can list many features. Check each one against the specification section before you count it.
