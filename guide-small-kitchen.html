Measure your counter first

Depth decides whether it fits under cabinets; width decides whether it crowds the sink. From listing dimensions:

  • Chefman 8QT basket: 12.75D x 13.5W in - shallowest at 32.4 cm depth, 172.1 sq in footprint.
  • Chefman 6QT dual: 13.38D x 11.81W in - narrowest at 34.0 cm depth, 158.0 sq in footprint.
  • Emeril 26QT oven: 16.9D x 15.8W in - deepest at 42.9 cm depth, 267.0 sq in footprint.

Dimensions as shown on each Amazon listing on 2026-11-19. Footprint = width x depth; derived by us, not a manufacturer claim. Method and limits in cleanup & counter-space guide.

Questions

Which air fryer fits the smallest counter?

By depth, the Chefman 8QT (32.4 cm). By footprint, the Chefman 6QT dual-basket (158.0 sq in). Measure both depth and width before buying.

Is capacity per centimetre a manufacturer claim?

No. It is derived by us from listing dimensions (inches x 2.54 = cm) and title capacity. Treat it as a planning aid, verify fit on the listing.
