Design suggestions - feed these into the visual design prompt

These are the concrete art-direction inputs for SG-11. They describe what "a polished marketing affiliate site, not AI slop" means for this repository, so a design pass can be reviewed against them before any CSS ships.

#Design inputWhy it counters the "generated" lookConstraint
D-1Publication masthead with wordmark, tagline and issue date (verification date), not a generic app barReal shops and magazines have a masthead; app bars read as scaffoldingMust keep disclosure strip directly beneath
D-2Editorial hero: one strong headline, one lede, two CTAs (shop / read) - no badge soupAudits open with process; shops open with the offerBadges limited to three: verified date, sponsored labeled, price-snapshot warning
D-3Product-card anatomy: image slot, name, star+count, price with list-price strike and snapshot date, 3 spec chips, one CTA, sponsored microcopyCard = conversion unit; prose bullets inside cards are the biggest AI-slop tellNo product photo until SG-04 decides; use licensed placeholder or typographic tile

D-5Color: warm paper background, one accent for CTAs, one for trust/verification, muted ink - not the default blue/purple gradientGradients + glow = generated; flat warm palette = printed catalogueContrast AA on all text (WCAG)
D-6Guide layout: key-takeaways box above the fold, at-a-glance table, honest "what we cannot tell you" callout, related-product rail at the endOpinionated structure reads as edited; wall-of-text reads as generatedEvery spec cell links to its listing (existing rule)
D-7Seasonal skin layer (holiday gift-guide banner) that sits on top of the base design and can be switched offShows the site is maintained on a calendar, like a real publicationDated price snapshots only (D-19); no "trending" without a source
