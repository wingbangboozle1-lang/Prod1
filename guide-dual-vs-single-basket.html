Two baskets or one: what dual-zone cooking actually changes

By The Countertop Guide editorial desk · Listing data checked 2026-11-20 · Spec-based, not hands-on tested how we work

The same quart figure can mean two very different cooking setups. A dual-basket unit cooks two dishes at once; a single basket cooks one. Here is what each shape actually offers, exactly as the listings state it.

On this page: The picks – Full comparison – Questions readers ask

What each shape offers, as listed

Dual-basket (Chefman 6QT, B0CDY277D3). Two independent 3-quart baskets with Sync Finish, so two dishes can finish together; the listing describes dual-zone cooking for different dishes at the same time.

Single basket (Chefman 8QT, B08DKYBTPH). One 8-quart basket with a 450°F Hi-Fry option and a top-rack dishwasher-safe basket and tray.

Oven cavity (Emeril 26QT, B09B7SB46R). A single large cavity the listing describes as suitable for 4-6 people, a 6-lb rotisserie chicken and a 12-inch pizza.

When two baskets earn their depth

  • You cook two different dishes at once, at different temperatures or times.
  • Sync Finish matters to you: the listing says the two baskets can finish at the same time.
  • You want to air-fry a main and a side without waiting, and you have the counter depth (34.0 cm for the Chefman 6QT).

When one big basket is simpler

  • You cook one dish at a time and want the shallowest footprint (32.4 cm for the Chefman 8QT).
  • Simpler cleanup: one basket and tray rather than two.
  • You fry in large single batches - fries for the whole family - where one 8-quart cavity is the point.

What the listing cannot tell you

Whether two baskets actually save you time, or whether a single large basket crisps a big batch better, is a hands-on question. We have not cooked in any of these units, so we do not rank them on cooking performance - only on the listed form, capacity and care facts above.

Questions readers ask

What is the difference between dual-basket and single-basket air fryers?

A dual-basket unit cooks in two independent baskets that can run different dishes at once (the Chefman 6QT lists Sync Finish); a single-basket unit cooks in one cavity (the Chefman 8QT is 8 quarts).

Can the Chefman 6QT cook two dishes at different times?

Yes, per the listing: two independent 3-quart baskets with dual-zone cooking, so different dishes can cook at the same time.

Is a 26-quart oven the same as two baskets?

No. The Emeril 26QT is one large oven cavity, not two baskets. The listing describes it for a whole chicken and pizza; it is a different cooking setup from a dual-basket fryer.
