Disclosures & policy checks in the publishing workflow

Disclosure above the fold on every page, in the Agreement’s own words, and repeated in the footer. Every outbound product link - static or script-built - is marked sponsored and labelled. Volatile Program Content is not displayed at all, because a static build cannot refresh it inside 24 hours. Endorsement context is reviewed against the Operating Agreement and FTC guidance before publishing.

Our disclosure (verbatim, every page)

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Prices and availability are snapshots from Amazon.com on the verification date and can change - always check the price on Amazon before you buy.

Rendered from data/products.json → site.disclosure, the same field the strip and the footer read, so this page cannot drift from what the site actually says. The sentence up to the first full stop is the one Operating Agreement section 5 requires.

How each outbound link is marked

  • URL format: https://www.amazon.com/dp/ASIN?tag=shopnow047c-20 (official listing URL + tracking ID).
  • Attributes: rel="sponsored nofollow noopener", target="_blank", visible "sponsored" label and "Opens amazon.com" text so destination is never obscured.
  • Short links are recorded in the verification register as submitted links with observed redirect targets in a separate field. They are not used as outbound buttons.

Ratings, badges and “bought in past month” counts are volatile Program Content, so they left the shop pages with the prices. A refresh job is the only route back (SG-20).

Reader capture, if ever approved (SG-07), may only cover new guides.
