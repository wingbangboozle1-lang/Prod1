Start with the question, not the machine

Two dishes

Do you cook two different things in one evening?

Then the dual-basket machine is the only one here built for it: two independent 3-quart baskets with Sync Finish, per its listing. A single basket means cooking in rounds.

A crowd

Are you feeding four to six, or roasting whole birds?

The 26-quart French-door oven is the one whose listing makes those claims - 4 to 6 people, a 6-lb rotisserie chicken, a 12-inch pizza. Confirm them on the listing before you buy them.

Space

Is your counter depth the limit?

Measure it in inches, then compare: 13.38, 16.9 and 12.75. The shallowest is the 8-quart basket. Four inches of difference decides whether a unit lives on the counter or in a cupboard.
