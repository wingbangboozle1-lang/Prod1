A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity figures can be hard to turn into a real meal plan. Here’s a simple checklist for reading what manufacturers say—and spotting what the number alone cannot tell you.

Find the exact unit and definition

Record the number and unit exactly as the official listing presents them. Check whether it describes a basket, cavity, or another measure. Do not compare figures across formats unless the manufacturer definitions are comparable.

Connect capacity to your portions

Write down what you cook most often and for how many people. A listed volume does not guarantee a particular batch size, cooking result, or serving count.

Compare dimensions too

Capacity without exterior dimensions tells only part of the story. Measure available counter depth, width, height, and any manufacturer-required clearance. Consider storage when it is not in use.

Check the source and the date

Product pages can change. Keep the official source URL and date you checked it. If details are missing or contradictory, leave the comparison cell blank and flag it.

  • Exact capacity value and unit copied from an official source
  • Meaning of the capacity explained or marked unknown
  • Exterior dimensions checked against your available space
  • Typical meal and household needs written down
  • Source and verification date recorded
