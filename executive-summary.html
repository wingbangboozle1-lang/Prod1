What the record supports

  • Earlier repository notes record five submitted short-link destinations and Amazon listing captures dated 2026-11-19. The observed ASINs and listing URLs are kept as observations; they do not establish the intended product.
  • Each product-intent record now has separate fields for intended ASIN, official intended listing URL, confirmation date, and confirmation source. Those fields are blank pending confirmation; see the register and FLG-013.
  • The root publication has unique page titles/descriptions and self-canonical URLs. The published sitemap lists buyer-facing pages; RSS is generated from the guide manifest. Internal decision pages remain reachable from the footer but are not submitted for indexing.
  • Every outbound Amazon product link carries rel="sponsored". The client/server event contract and local endpoint tests are aligned; the endpoint is not hosted, so no site-wide event data is being collected.

Open verification and compliance gates

  • Associates Central: the exact compliance/attribution status of the submitted short links is unresolved. A tagged link per confirmed product still needs a destination check and a tracking-report check. We do not claim the supplied links are invalid.
  • Product data: prices and ratings are time-sensitive historical snapshots; thermometer dimensions and some appliance fields are missing. Re-check official sources before keeping or refreshing these values. API/content permissions were not freshly reviewed here.
  • Search Console: the public site URL and sitemap are defined, but account ownership, indexing, and query reports are not confirmed.
  • Measurement: only browser-local aggregate counts work while the endpoint URL is blank. A separate HTTPS host and a final hosting/privacy decision are required; conversions and revenue must come from authorized first-party reports.
  • Photography and testing: no product photos or hands-on test claims are used. Use original photography only after rights and permissions are documented; publish testing claims only after the test is actually done.

Editorial path

Proposed four-week pilot: publish one sourced, question-led guide per week, then review aggregated Search Console queries monthly once the property is verified. Current titles are editorial hypotheses, not measured demand or popularity claims. The campaign ledger starts empty; no orders, conversion score, or revenue forecast is shown.
