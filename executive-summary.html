Executive summary

What this site is, what is verified, and what happens next

The Countertop Guide is a spec-backed affiliate publication for two kitchen categories - air fryers and meat thermometers - built from five Amazon listings verified on 2026-11-19. It leads with products and buyer questions, not with process. Every number links to its source.

What is verified

  • Each submitted short link resolves to the ASIN and official listing URL in verification register (intended ASIN kept separate from observed redirect target).
  • Titles, dimensions (air fryers), capacity, special features and bullets as shown on Amazon.com on 2026-11-19. Price and rating were observed on the same date and are recorded in the register, not displayed on shop pages - see FLG-032 for the policy reason.
  • Compliance posture: disclosure above the fold on every page and in the footer; every outbound product link is rel="sponsored" and labeled; no trend or popularity claims without a dated snapshot; no hands-on testing claims.

What is not verified (and therefore not claimed)

  • Thermometer dimensions (not stated on either listing), the Emeril warranty period (a PDF is linked, nothing on the page), the CHEF iQ +/-F accuracy figure, and the Chefman 8QT wattage, where one listing disagrees with itself - all blank or attributed, and flagged.
  • Short-link compliance status (unresolved), Special Link test in Associates Central (manual gate), commission rates (not published here), search volume / popularity (not claimed).
  • Product photography: no Amazon image is used anywhere; the drawings and the hero illustration on the pages are ours and labelled as such. A licence for real photographs is still undecided (SG-04), and whether a reader could take a line drawing for the product itself is an open question (Q-31).

How money and measurement work

Outbound links use tracking ID shopnow047c-20 and are marked sponsored. Click and conversion measurement is defined (five events) with a privacy-preserving local counter; the self-hosted endpoint passes its contract test in CI (scripts/test_event_contract.py) and was exercised locally on 2026-11-20, but is not deployed. The campaign ledger starts empty - orders and revenue are recorded only from authorized first-party reports. See Method & campaign ledger.

What the 2026-11-19 pass changed

  • Reader-facing order. Front page now leads with the picks and the products; the register, flags and method moved behind one Review desk link and the footer.
  • No stale figures. Price, rating, rating count, badge and “bought in past month” are off the shop pages, with the policy read behind the decision (FLG-032).
  • Disclosure wording. The strip and footer carry the Operating Agreement’s sentence verbatim, plus a plain-language line a reader can parse.
  • Findability. Per-page canonical, unique titles and descriptions, absolute raster og:image, generated sitemap and feed, ItemList/Article/Breadcrumb structured data, and the canonical-vs-sitemap mismatch closed.
  • Links. Script-built chooser links now carry rel="sponsored" and a visible label, and the CI gate checks the JavaScript too.
  • Gate in the pipeline. scripts/check_meta.mjs runs before the Pages upload, which also closes request R-01 from session b4c6c619.
  • New surfaces. about.html (who writes this, how it earns), review-desk.html (every internal surface in one group) and a seasonal guide that demonstrates the cadence pipeline.

What the 2026-11-20 pass changed

Counts on this page are read from the build, not remembered: 32 pages, 12 guides, 11 calendar topics, 51 rows on the flag list, 50 suggestions and 31 questions on the decision desk.

  • The pages had no skin. Forty-four class names used by the generator had no rule in assets/style.css, and nine custom properties the decision-desk block referenced were never declared - which is most of why a well-populated page read as a raw register. A component layer now covers the product card, hero, picks, tiles, calendar, guide typography and desk rows, and the missing tokens are aliased.
  • Original artwork, ours. Five line drawings of the appliance forms (dual basket, French door, single basket, handheld, wireless probes) sit on the product cards and tiles, with a commissioned illustration opening the front page. Every one is captioned as our drawing, not product photography; no Amazon image is stored, linked or imitated. Product photographs remain gated by SG-04.
  • Three guides shipped from the topic calendar: counter height and cabinet fit, what is actually in the box, and how to check a price claim. The guide list is now one source of truth: the same data builds the guides index, the front-page module, the footer column and the RSS feed, and the build fails if they disagree.
  • Dates separated. A guide’s publish date (data/editorial.json → dates.publish) is no longer the same field as the register’s verification date, so a page written today against a listing read last week says both, truthfully, in the markup and in the feed.
  • The measurement machinery is now tested, not described. The endpoint and the browser client disagreed on three of the five event names; they now share one contract, and scripts/test_event_contract.py runs in CI on every push. The endpoint still is not hosted and the site still sends nothing.
  • The decision desk is usable. All 81 numbered rows carry a pick control (was 6), and the export writes a dated Markdown decision sheet a reviewer pastes into docs/decisions.md. Picks never leave the browser.
  • SERP hygiene. Four guide titles and one description that exceeded search-result length were trimmed; scripts/check_meta.mjs now reports 0 warnings where it reported 5.

Next gates (owner decisions)

In order, because each one unblocks a different thing: Q-30 (what counts as an approval on the desk, and where it is written down) → SG-01 (the Special Link test, which needs your Associates login) → SG-02 (canonical domain plus Search Console, the only route to measured queries) → SG-03 (whether to host the counter this pass made trustworthy) → SG-04 (photography licence). See the decision desk, Flagged for review and the 2026-11-20 re-verification record.

Two limits on this page, stated rather than buried: nothing here is a forecast of traffic, ranking or revenue, and no session has yet seen a real query or a real click. The site’s evidence is that five listings were read carefully and the machinery around them was checked. Whether buyers arrive is the next question, not a claim.
