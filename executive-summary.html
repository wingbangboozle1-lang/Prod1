What is verified

  • Each submitted short link resolves to the ASIN and official listing URL in verification register (intended ASIN kept separate from observed redirect target).
  • Titles, ratings, price snapshots, dimensions (air fryers), capacity, special features and bullets as shown on Amazon.com on 2026-11-19.
  • Compliance posture: disclosure above the fold on every page and in the footer (Operating Agreement Section 5 wording); every outbound product link is rel="sponsored" and labeled; no popularity claims without a dated snapshot; no hands-on testing claims.

What is not verified (and therefore not claimed)

  • Short-link compliance status (unresolved), Special Link test in Associates Central (manual gate), commission rates (not published here), search volume / popularity (not claimed).
  • Product photography (no Amazon images used; real photography needs a licence decision - SG-04).
