What is verified

  • Each submitted short link resolves to the ASIN and official listing URL in the verification register; intended ASIN and observed redirect target are kept in separate fields and never merged.
  • Titles, ratings, price snapshots (buy box and list price), dimensions (air fryers), capacity fields, special features, bullets and policy text re-read against the live Amazon listings and the Associates Operating Agreement on 2026-11-19 (integration pass).
  • Compliance posture: the exact Operating Agreement Section 5 disclosure sentence above the fold on every page and in the footer; every outbound product link rel="sponsored" and labeled; no "trending" or popularity claims without a dated snapshot; no hands-on testing claims.
  • Trust machinery: a publishing gate (scripts/check_site.mjs) runs in the Pages workflow before deploy; five privacy-preserving analytics events are defined and counted locally.

What is not verified (and therefore not claimed)

  • Thermometer dimensions and any field not shown on the listing - blank and flagged (FLG-004).
  • Short-link compliance status (unresolved, FLG-001), the Special Link test in Associates Central (manual gate), commission rates, search volume / popularity.
  • One on-listing conflict: Chefman 8QT wattage shows 1000W (bullets) and 1700W (Product information) on the same page - both shown, flagged (FLG-022).
  • Product photography (no Amazon images used; real photography needs a licence decision - FLG-009).

How money and measurement work

Outbound links use tracking ID shopnow047c-20 and are marked sponsored. Click and conversion measurement is defined (five events: page_view, outbound_click, search, chooser_complete, guide_read) with a privacy-preserving local counter; the self-hosted endpoint ships as code in analytics-endpoint/ and needs a hosting decision before data flows. The campaign ledger starts empty - orders and revenue are recorded only from authorized first-party reports. See Method & campaign ledger.

Next gates (owner decisions at the review desk)

Special Link validation, analytics hosting, photography licence, Search Console verification, and the ranked suggestions. Nothing scales until those gates pass. Start at the review desk; the design direction is captured in DESIGN_PROMPT.md; open items live on Flagged for review.
