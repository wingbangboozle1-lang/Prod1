How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

By The Countertop Guide editors · Spec-based guide (not hands-on tested) · Listing data verified 2026-11-19 · 4 min read

The best air fryer is the one that fits your counter and that you will actually wash. Here is the footprint math and the cleanup facts as listed - nothing more.

On this page: Shortlist from verified listings · Full comparison · Questions

Measure twice

Depth decides fit under cabinets; width decides whether it crowds the sink. From listing dimensions:

  • Chefman 8QT basket: 12.75D x 13.5W in - shallowest, 172.1 sq in footprint.
  • Chefman 6QT dual: 13.38D x 11.81W in - narrowest, 158.0 sq in footprint.
  • Emeril 26QT oven: 16.9D x 15.8W in - deepest and widest, 267.0 sq in footprint.

Capacity per centimetre of counter depth (derived): Emeril 0.61, Chefman 8QT 0.25, Chefman 6QT dual 0.18 qt/cm. The oven gives the most capacity per centimetre of depth - if you have the depth to spare.

Cleanup as listed

  • Chefman 6QT dual: removable non-stick baskets are dishwasher safe (per bullets).
  • Chefman 8QT: 8-quart non-stick basket and flat tray are top-rack dishwasher safe (per bullets).
  • Emeril 26QT: six accessories included per the title (the listing description says seven - see FLG-021); check the listing for each accessory's care instructions before buying.

"Dishwasher safe" in a bullet is the seller's claim. We have not washed any of these baskets. If dishwasher care is decisive, confirm the exact wording on the listing.

Questions readers ask

Which air fryer takes the least counter space?

By footprint, the Chefman 6QT dual-basket (158.0 sq in). By depth, the Chefman 8QT (32.4 cm). Measure both depth and width.

Are the baskets dishwasher safe?

Chefman 6QT: baskets dishwasher safe per bullets. Chefman 8QT: basket and tray top-rack dishwasher safe per bullets. Confirm on the listing.

How is capacity per cm calculated?

Title capacity (quarts) divided by depth in centimetres (inches x 2.54). Derived by us from listing dimensions; not a manufacturer claim.
