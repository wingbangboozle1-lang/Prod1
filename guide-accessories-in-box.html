Air fryer accessories: what actually comes in the box?

By The Countertop Guide editorial desk · Listing data checked 2026-11-20 · Spec-based, not hands-on tested how we work

An accessory is a promise about your first week: a rack means dehydrating, a spit means Sunday, a second basket means no staggering. So it matters that a listing does not always agree with itself about the count. One of the five listings on this site does exactly that, and it is the reason this guide exists.

On this page: The picks – Full comparison – Questions readers ask

One listing, two counts

The Emeril 26QT French-door listing (B09B7SB46R) says Six Cooking Accessories Included in its title, re-read on 2026-11-20 and still there. The same page’s item-details table says the configuration is an air fryer toaster oven combo with 24 functions and 7 accessories, and its product description also says seven premium accessories. So one page carries six in the title and seven twice below it. We are not going to pick the answer we prefer: all three readings are on the record on our flag list, each labelled with the field it came from. What you do with it is the useful part.

If a specific piece matters to you, the accessory count is the wrong thing to read. Counts can group a rack and a tray as one item, or split a basket from its crisper plate. Ask for the named list.

Where a named list actually lives

  1. The included-components field. On the ThermoMaven instant-read (B0DHZTCNR5) that field states four items: the thermometer, one AAA battery, a user manual and a calibration certificate. Short, specific, and the kind of line a title never carries.
  2. The bullets. The CHEF iQ Sense (B0DZFD6R9Z) item details say the box holds three probes and a hub, and the bullets describe a hub that charges all three at once. The title states the sensor count in its own way, so read both and note the difference rather than smoothing it over.
  3. The manual or a seller question. For the Emeril, that is the only way to close a six-versus-seven gap. It is a two-minute message, and it is the answer you can quote when the box arrives.

What we did not capture

None of the five listings we read gave a complete accessories manifest beyond the fields above, so our tables do not invent one. The parts that are on the record: the Chefman 6QT (B0CDY277D3) ships two independent 3-quart baskets, listed as dishwasher-safe and removable; the Chefman 8QT (B08DKYBTPH) bullets describe a basket and tray that are top-rack dishwasher safe; the Emeril functions include rotisserie, which implies a spit and something to hold it, and the listing states rotisserie use for a 6-lb chicken.

That is the honest shape of the answer: the basket is on the record, the spare rack is not. Anyone writing the reverse from a memory of a similar model is guessing.

Six questions before you decide a kit is worth it

  • Which single piece do you expect to use in week one? If the answer is the basket, an accessory count is decoration.
  • Does each piece fit the cavity, not just the box? A 12-inch pizza is a claim about the inside (the listing makes it); a rack you cannot insert is a claim about nothing.
  • Dishwasher or hand wash? The 6QT baskets are listed dishwasher-safe; a rotisserie spit rarely is, and the listings here do not say.
  • Can you buy the missing piece later? Nonstick baskets and racks are usually sold separately by the brand; a proprietary cage is not.
  • Does the function need the accessory, or the setting? Dehydrating on the Emeril is one of the functions the listing names; the trays are what make it practical.
  • Is the number you are reading in the title, the item details or the description? Note which, because that is where a listing disagrees with itself.

Then read the presets, the wattage and the warranty against the same page: the multi-function checklist walks those fields on this exact oven, and the capacity guide covers what the 26-quart figure can and cannot mean.

Questions readers ask

How many accessories come with the Emeril 26QT air fryer?

The listing itself gives two answers: the title says six cooking accessories, and both the item-details table and the product description say seven. We publish both and flag the conflict instead of choosing one. A named list from the seller settles it.

Do air fryer accessories need to be dishwasher-safe?

It is worth checking per piece. Here, the Chefman 6QT baskets are listed as dishwasher-safe and the 8QT basket and tray are listed top-rack safe; nothing on these listings states a figure for roasting racks or spit parts.

Can you buy a missing air fryer basket separately?

Brands commonly sell replacement baskets and racks for a given model, but nothing on the five listings we captured states a spare-parts offer. Treat it as a question for the seller, not as an assumption.
