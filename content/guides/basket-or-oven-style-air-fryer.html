Two formats appear among the supplied listings: a drawer-style basket and a countertop oven with a door and racks. The choice changes how much food fits, how you load it, and what you can cook without extra steps. This guide lists the questions to settle first. It does not say which format performs better.

What the listings show

The three air fryers in our table use two formats, and their titles say so:

  • Basket style: the Chefman 6 Quart listing describes "Dual Basket" with "2 Independent 3Qt" baskets; the Chefman 8 Qt listing describes an "XL Nonstick Dishwasher-Safe Basket" with a "TurboFry 4-in-1" function set. (Chefman 6 Quart listing; Chefman 8 Qt listing)
  • Oven style: the Emeril Lagasse listing describes a "French Door Multi-Function Air Fryer Toaster Oven" with "Extra Large Interior" and "Six Cooking Accessories Included". (Emeril Lagasse listing)

Titles are a starting point. Confirm dimensions, included parts and warranty on the listing itself before you decide.

A simple way to decide

  1. Write down the largest single item you cook most often.
  2. If it fits a basket in one flat layer, a basket format is the simpler test.
  3. If you need trays, racks or baking, shortlist oven-style units and check their footprint against your counter.
  4. Compare the two shortlisted listings on the same questions above, using only the listing's stated figures.

What this guide does not cover
