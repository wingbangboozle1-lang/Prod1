Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

By The Countertop Guide editors · Spec-based guide (not hands-on tested) · Listing data verified 2026-11-19 · 4 min read

Multi-function ovens promise everything. Before you pay for presets you will never use, ask these questions. We apply them to the Emeril 26QT French-door oven.

On this page: Shortlist from verified listings · Full comparison · Questions

The pre-purchase questions

  1. How many presets, and do you recognise them? Emeril title says 24 One-Touch presets; bullets say 25 cooking pre-sets. That discrepancy is flagged - count on the listing, not the headline. Functions shown: Air Fry, Convection Bake, Rotisserie, Toast, Grill, Dehydrate, Roast, Slow Cook, Broil.
  2. What accessories are included? The title says six cooking accessories and the item-details field lists six (rack, bake pan, drip tray, crisper basket, rotisserie spit, fetch tool) - but the product description says seven. Flagged as FLG-021. List them on the listing before buying; replacements cost extra.
  3. What is the wattage? The Emeril's Product information table lists 1700 watts / 120 volts (read 2026-11-19). Note that the Chefman 8QT listing on this site gives two different wattage figures - see the wattage guide and check yours.
  4. What does the warranty say? Chefman listings state 1-year assurance/warranty; CHEF iQ states 2-year assurance. For the Emeril, the listing links a warranty PDF without stating a term in the page text - open the PDF before buying.
  5. Does it fit? 16.9D x 15.8W x 14.8H in is the largest of the three fryers. French doors need swing clearance.

Rule of thumb: pay for functions you will use weekly (air fry, bake, toast, rotisserie), not for the longest preset list.
