Before you compare models

  1. List the cooking tasks you actually expect to use.
  2. Check the current listing and manual for accessory names and care.
  3. Measure the counter and verify electrical and clearance requirements from the manufacturer.
  4. Separate listing claims from tested performance. This publication has not tested the Emeril oven.
