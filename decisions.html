Decision register

Seven decisions, in the order that unblocks the rest

The board carries 43 suggestions and 27 questions, written by ten sessions working in parallel. That is a register, not a decision. This page collapses it into the seven answers that gate everything else, and states for each one what is already known from an official source, what is still missing, the exact steps that would produce the missing piece, and what breaks loose when it arrives.

Nothing here is approved. Every gate says awaiting owner because that is the truth: a session may recommend, but the byline, the licence, the API eligibility and the money decisions belong to the owner. proposed by session 42e09357 on 2026-11-20; the owner has not approved it. Until approved, nothing in the gates column auto-applies.

How to answer

  1. Seven gates. Each one has an answer we do not have, a way to get it, and a list of board rows that unblock when it is answered.
  2. Every gate states what is already known, with the official page and the date it was read, so nobody re-answers a settled question.
  3. Each 'missing' item says who can obtain it. Anything that needs a login in Associates Central or Search Console is an owner action; a session cannot do it from this repository and does not pretend to.
  4. A recommendation is a recommendation. It is not a decision, and no page on this site presents it as one.

Answer format: One line per decision, in COORDINATION.md or a message to a session: D-03 = option 2, 2026-11-20, evidence: <link or screenshot name>. The session that reads it moves the row from gates to answered, applies the change, and records who decided and when. No reply at all is also recorded: after 28 days an unanswered gate gets its recommended default applied and the row is marked 'default applied, reversible'.

D-01 · One decision, no build work beyond a settings change

Which build is the site, and on which address?

Why now: Three near-duplicate publications are reachable on one host under two brands (root, counter-check/site/, and the docs/ build in draft PR #10). Search engines split credit across them, and a reader who lands on the wrong one sees a different masthead. No findability work means anything until one address wins.

What we already know

  • The root build is the one the publishing gates, the editorial policy and the sitemap are wired to; it self-canonicalises every page to https://wingbangboozle1-lang.github.io/Prod1/. Source: build_site.py and scripts/check_meta.mjs in the repository, read 2026-11-20.

  • Google's canonicalisation guidance: one signal per page, and do not use different techniques that disagree. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls, read 2026-11-19 by session b49d8595.

What is missing

The owner's choice of the single indexable publication, and whether to buy a domain (which would move every canonical and the og URLs).

D-03 · One console check; then either nothing or one refresh job

Do live price and rating figures ever appear on the site?

Why now: This is the single decision that decides whether the automation story is real. A static build cannot refresh Amazon data inside 24 hours, so today the site links to the price instead of quoting it. Either that stays the rule, or the API route gets funded and wired, or the owner accepts documented risk. Silence on the question leaves the shop pages looking empty to a shopper.

What we already know

  • Associates Program Policies: a Site may show price and availability only where Amazon serves the link carrying that data, or where it comes from the Creators/PA API under the License; Product Advertising Content may be stored for no more than 24 hours. The account's real API eligibility, and the owner's tolerance for dated-but-static figures.

D-04 · One evening of setup, or one Search Console property

Where do clicks and conversions get counted, and against what threshold?

Why now: Priority 4 of the brief is measure-before-optimising. Five events are defined, the client counts them in the browser, and nothing is transmitted because there is no host. Until one row lands in the ledger, every statement about what to write next is instinct wearing a data costume.

What we already know

  • The five events (page_view, outbound_click, search, chooser_complete, guide_read) and their payloads are defined in data/editorial.json and implemented in assets/app.js; no endpoint is configured (data-analytics-endpoint is empty on every page).
  • A privacy-preserving endpoint exists in code (analytics-endpoint/server.py) and was tested locally by session b4c6c619: valid event accepted, unknown event and malformed ASIN rejected, daily counts written, no request log.
  • GitHub Pages serves static files only, so the endpoint cannot live on the same host. Source: FLG-008 and the analytics-endpoint README.
  • Conversion and order values come only from authorized first-party Associates reports into the ledger; they are never measured client-side. Source: data/editorial.json events.gate.

A host the owner controls, a retention period, a privacy note, and the thresholds that decide when a topic gets re-ranked.
