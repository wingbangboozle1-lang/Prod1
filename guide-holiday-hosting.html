The holiday hosting test: which air fryer actually feeds a crowd?

By The Countertop Guide editors · Spec-based guide (not hands-on tested) · Listing data verified 2026-11-19 · 6 min read

Thanksgiving week, Hanukkah, game day, the office cookie swap - December asks more of a countertop appliance than a Tuesday does. Here is how our three verified fryers answer the hosting questions that actually decide the meal: how much fits, how many dishes run at once, and whether it fits the counter when the mixer is already there.

On this page: Shortlist from verified listings · Full comparison · Questions

The three hosting questions

  1. How much actually fits? The Emeril 26QT listing claims a 6-lb rotisserie chicken and a 12-inch pizza, and describes serving 4-6 people (seller claim, per bullets). The Chefman 6QT splits into two 3-quart baskets - two side dishes at once with Sync Finish. The Chefman 8QT is one 8-quart cavity for a single big dish.
  2. How many dishes can run at once? Only the dual-basket cooks two different foods on independent settings. The oven wins on volume per dish; the dual-basket wins on dish count.
  3. Will it still fit when the counter is full? December counters host pie boxes and cutting boards. Depth is the binding constraint: 32.4 cm (Chefman 8QT), 34.0 cm (Chefman 6QT dual), 42.9 cm (Emeril oven). Measure before the guests arrive.
