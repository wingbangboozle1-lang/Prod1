Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

By The Countertop Guide editorial desk · Listing data checked 2026-11-20 · Spec-based, not hands-on tested how we work

If you cook two different dishes at the same time, a dual-basket wins. If you cook for a crowd or want rotisserie and a 12-inch pizza, an oven-style unit wins. Everything else is footprint and cleanup. Here is how to decide, using the three fryers on this site.

On this page: The picks – Full comparison – Questions readers ask

Start with the job, not the brand

Dual-basket (Chefman 6QT, B0CDY277D3). Two independent 3-quart baskets with Sync Finish, so two dishes can finish together. The listing describes dual-zone cooking for different dishes simultaneously. Depth is 13.38 inches (34.0 cm) - the middle of the three.

Oven-style French-door (Emeril 26QT, B09B7SB46R). Built for volume: the listing describes 26-quart capacity for 4-6 people, rotisserie for a 6-lb chicken, and baking a 12-inch pizza. It is also the deepest at 16.9 inches (42.9 cm) and the largest footprint (267.0 sq in). French doors open with a single pull.

Single-basket (Chefman 8QT, B08DKYBTPH). One large basket with a 450F Hi-Fry option and a top-rack dishwasher-safe basket and tray. Shallowest at 12.75 inches (32.4 cm). Best when counter depth is the constraint and you cook one dish at a time.

Three questions that decide it

  1. Do you cook two things at once? If yes, dual-basket. If no, single-basket or oven-style.
  2. Do you cook for 4-6 or want rotisserie/pizza? If yes, oven-style. The Emeril listing makes those specific claims; verify them on the listing before buying.
  3. What is your counter depth? Measure it. The Emeril needs 42.9 cm; the Chefman 8QT needs 32.4 cm. A 10 cm difference decides fit.

What the listing cannot tell you

We have not cooked in any of these units, so we do not claim crispness, noise, or "lived-with" durability. Those require hands-on testing we have not done - see Flagged for review. What we can tell you is form, capacity, footprint and cleanup exactly as the listing states them. Price and rating live on Amazon and move, so we link to them instead of quoting them.

Questions readers ask

Which is better, basket or oven-style air fryer?

Neither - they do different jobs. Dual-basket for two dishes at once, oven-style for crowds, rotisserie and pizza, single-basket for compact one-dish cooking. Use the three questions above.

How deep is the Emeril 26QT oven?

16.9 inches (42.9 cm) deep, per the Amazon listing dimensions (16.9D x 15.8W x 14.8H in). Measure your counter before buying.

Can the Chefman 6QT cook two dishes at once?

Yes, per the listing: two independent 3-quart baskets with Sync Finish so both can finish at the same time.
