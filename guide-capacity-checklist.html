Guide - Capacity

A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

By The Countertop Guide editorial desk · Listing data checked 2026-11-20 · Spec-based, not hands-on tested how we work

Capacity numbers are the easiest place to be misled. Quarts vs litres, single vs dual baskets, and 'feeds 4-6' all mean different things. Use this checklist on any listing - we apply it to the three fryers here.

On this page: The picks – Full comparison – Questions readers ask

The checklist

  1. Which number is the title, which is the field? Chefman 6QT title vs "6 liters" field; Emeril 26QT title vs "24.6 liters" field. Note both; do not silently convert. See Flagged FLG-002.
  2. Single or split? "6 Quart" that is 2 x 3 Qt behaves differently from a single 6 Qt basket. The Chefman dual-basket is explicitly 2 x 3 Qt.
  3. What food claim backs the number? Emeril: 4-6 people, 6-lb rotisserie chicken, 12-inch pizza (per bullets). Chefman 8QT: family-size single basket (per bullets). Ask: what food, what portion?
  4. What is the footprint cost? Capacity per square inch: Emeril 0.097, Chefman 8QT 0.046, Chefman 6QT dual 0.038 qt/sq in (derived from listing dims). Bigger is not automatically better if it does not fit.
  5. What is missing? The Emeril warranty period is not stated on the listing page (a PDF is linked). Wattage is listed at 1700 W / 120 V. The Chefman 8QT disagrees with itself, so read the figure on the page you buy from.

Worked example

Two baskets of 3 Qt each (Chefman 6QT dual) let you run chicken in one and vegetables in the other, finishing together. A single 8 Qt basket (Chefman 8QT) gives one large cavity for one dish. A 26QT oven (Emeril) gives a cavity for a whole chicken and pizza. Same word - "capacity" - three different cooking experiences.

Questions readers ask

Is 6 quarts the same as 6 litres?

No. The Chefman 6QT listing title says 6 Quart while its capacity field says 6 liters. We report both exactly as shown and flag the difference rather than converting silently.

How many people does the Emeril 26QT feed?

The listing bullets describe it as suitable for 4-6 people. That is the seller's description, not our tested serving count.

What does capacity per square inch mean?

Title capacity divided by footprint (width x depth from listing dimensions). It shows how much capacity you get per unit of counter. Derived by us; see compare page for method.
