Review desk

Everything internal, in one place

This is the counter behind the counter: the register, the open questions, the flags, the method and the money rules. Reader-facing pages stay out of here on purpose - you will not find a row of status badges on the shop pages - but every one of these surfaces is reachable from the masthead’s Review desk link and from the footer of every page.

How to read a flag

A flag is not an admission of a defect in the product; it is a statement about our evidence. Needs decision means a human with account access must choose. Unverified means a value we could not confirm from an official source, left blank rather than guessed. Resolved items keep their row so the trail survives a rebuild.
