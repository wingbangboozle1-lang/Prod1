Executive summary

Counterwise is a publication-style front page and practical guide set for kitchen-appliance shopping. It leads with buyer questions rather than internal verification, and separates user-submitted links from product facts. It is a credible editorial foundation—not a live automated product-research or revenue-optimization platform.

What’s in place

Responsive site, disclosure strip and footer on each page, question-led guides, sitemap and RSS feed, an explicit evidence policy, and a review queue.

What isn’t established

Product identity or destination for five short links, Special Link/report validation, live analytics, Amazon API authorization, conversions, Search Console, and human product testing.

Current measurement

No analytics or sales metrics are active or claimed. GitHub Pages serves static files and cannot host the requested persistent self-hosted analytics endpoint by itself.

Suggestions queue · review before expanding

These are proposals, not silently adopted product or business decisions. Priorities reflect dependency and risk, not forecast revenue. Choose an item by recording an owner, evidence threshold, and decision date in the next coordination update.

SUGGESTION 01 · BLOCKER · LINKS & PRODUCT IDENTITY

Build a product-intent confirmation record for each submitted link

Keep the submitted URL, intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date, confirmation source, and observed redirect target in separate fields. Never overwrite intention with what a redirect happens to show.

Decision question

Can an enrolled Associates test confirm the intended listing and tracking report for each item?

Discover / gate

Test one link per product, capture source and timestamp, then approve expansion only after destination and authorized reporting are checked. Five records are blank/open today.

SUGGESTION 02 · HIGH · MEASUREMENT INFRASTRUCTURE

Choose the event design and host before writing analytics claims

Proposed minimal events: page_view, guide_view, outbound_click, email_opt_in, and affiliate_report_import. Avoid personal identifiers; use coarse page/link IDs, aggregate where possible, and document retention and consent. An email capture event is proposed only—the site has no form.

Decision question

Which first-party endpoint and storage host can the publisher operate, and what privacy/retention policy applies?

Discover / gate

Choose hosting and database; review legal/privacy requirements; test event handling, exclusion of personal data, deletion, and report provenance. Keep collection disabled until deployed and tested. GitHub Pages alone cannot receive and persist these events.

SUGGESTION 03 · HIGH · EDITORIAL TRUST

Write comparison copy only after evidence and experience exist

Keep useful, human-sounding question-led guides. For any named product comparison, cite each material fact to its exact official source and date, distinguish manufacturer claims from editorial experience, and publish firsthand observations only when a real test log exists.

Decision question

Will the publisher conduct original testing, use official specifications only, or use a clearly stated mix?

Discover / gate

Approve an editorial methodology and access to products before adding experiential claims or ranked recommendations.

SUGGESTION 04 · PUBLISHING CADENCE

Use a measured, sustainable editorial rhythm

Proposal: publish one useful guide every two weeks for the first eight weeks; review actual query impressions and reader feedback monthly. This is a planning hypothesis, not an optimal cadence claim. Do not auto-generate thin pages to meet a quota.

Decision question

Can an accountable editor source, review, and update one substantial guide per fortnight?

Discover / gate

Test capacity against the first four-guide cycle; adjust using documented research time, query evidence, and quality review.

SUGGESTION 05 · ORGANIC DISCOVERY

Measure question-led topics rather than labeling them “trending”

Use the published topics as working editorial hypotheses. Connect and verify Search Console, submit the sitemap, and log query/date/impressions/clicks before making popularity or growth claims. Add no fake volume estimates or search-rank promises.

Decision question

Who can verify the Search Console property and monitor query performance?

Discover / gate

Complete domain/property verification and confirm sitemap processing; then review query evidence after a meaningful data window.

SUGGESTION 06 · COMPLIANCE GATE

Require disclosure, endorsement-context, and API-policy review before publish

Keep the Associates statement visible under the masthead and repeat it in each footer. Review its wording and placement, the surrounding endorsement context, and any proposed program-content/API use against the current official rules. This page links source material for review; it is not legal advice or a completed legal audit.

SUGGESTION 07 · BRAND & OWNERSHIP

Approve the publication identity and a real byline before scaling

Counterwise is a working brand in this implementation. No human author identity, contact channel, or author credentials were supplied, so no person or hands-on expertise has been invented.

Decision question

Is the Counterwise name approved, who is accountable for the editorial content, and what reader contact route should be public?

Discover / gate

Confirm name, ownership, contact details, and accurate author credentials before adding an about page or named bylines.

SUGGESTION 08 · READER CAPTURE

Offer a genuinely optional email briefing only after the audience and service are ready

A reader-capture form is not live. First decide what the subscriber receives and how often, select a privacy-appropriate opt-in service, publish an accurate privacy notice, and provide unsubscribe handling. Do not collect an address into a dead form or promise a newsletter that has no owner.

Decision question

What useful recurring update would a reader want, and who will actually prepare and send it?

Discover / gate

Test reader interest only with a clear, consent-based proposal; choose a compliant provider and documented retention/unsubscribe flow before adding a form or email_opt_in event.
